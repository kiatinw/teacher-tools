Advanced Dot Point Draw // Mathematics Advanced, 2017 syllabus

draw a random content point · NESA Stage 6
how to play
  1. Draw a dot point and a mark value.
  2. Write one exam-style question on that dot point, pitched at that many marks.
  3. Screw your question into a ball.
  4. On "throw," lob it across the room.
  5. Grab a ball that lands near you and answer the question inside.
  6. Finished? Draw again and write your next one.
